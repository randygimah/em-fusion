Thesis figures and tables

Every figure and table planned for the thesis, numbered and captioned as it will appear in print. Items are drawn here first, then exported for Word. Numbers are provisional until each chapter is final.

Conventions used

  • Numbered by chapter: Figure 2.1, Table 2.1. Figure captions sit below the figure; table captions sit above the table.
  • Every item ends with its source: "Source: author" for new work, "Adapted from …" when redrawn from someone else's figure.
  • Figures are black and grey on white, so they print and photocopy cleanly.
  • One font, Arial, for every figure label, table and caption, so nothing changes style between items. To match a thesis template in another font, change one setting.
  • Tables are fully ruled: a line around every cell, with a heavier outer border and header rule. In the thesis, rebuild them as real Word tables rather than pasting images; "Copy table" pastes as an editable, ruled table.
  • Refer to every item in the text before it appears, and say what the reader should take from it.

Table 3.3DrawnSection 3.3, the RQ2 paragraph
Table 3.3: The five scenario types, what each stages on the radio and aircraft sides, and what the detection rules must conclude. Only a run on which the rules reach a conclusion produces an evidence bundle for the AI model to explain, so the five scenario types yield two kinds of bundle, and the sample for the blind marking is stratified on bundle kind. A plain negative control has nothing happening; a discriminating negative control has something real happening that no radio claim may be made about. The ambiguous mixed-evidence run is designed so that its cause cannot be resolved; detection is scored on naming the receiver without claiming interference, and whether each explanation recognises the ambiguity is judged in the blind marking.
